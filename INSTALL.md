# Install HAULS (no store needed)

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers on a computer.

1. Download **hauls-share-1.0.0.zip** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Unzip it into a folder you will keep (for example `Documents/HAULS`). Do not delete the folder later.
3. Open `chrome://extensions` (Edge: `edge://extensions`, Brave: `brave://extensions`).
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
5. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder.
6. Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and **pin** HAULS.

Open a product on Amazon.in, Flipkart or Myntra: a small HAULS tab appears on the right edge. Click it for the price history and verdict. Click the toolbar icon on a new tab for the full-page home (grocery prices and watchlist).

**Updating:** download the newer zip, unzip it over the same folder, then click the reload icon on the HAULS card in `chrome://extensions`.

**Notes**
- Chrome may show a "disable developer mode extensions" notice on startup: keep it enabled.
- Google sign-in is optional (it only syncs your watchlist across devices).
- Phones are not supported yet.

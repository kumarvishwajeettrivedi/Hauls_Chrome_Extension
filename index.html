Install

Ready in a minute.

No store needed. Works on a computer with Chrome, Edge or Brave.

  1. Download the zip and unzip it into a folder you'll keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode, the switch at the top right. Chrome uses it for extensions that are not from its store.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder, the one that contains manifest.json.
  4. Pin HAULS from the puzzle icon, then open a product on Amazon.in, Flipkart or Myntra and click the HAULS tab on the right edge.
Before you test: log in to the site you are inspecting (Amazon.in, Flipkart, Myntra or any other) so the page you test matches what you normally see. Then open a product, click the HAULS tab, and tell us what you find at hauls.support@gmail.com.
Updating: download the new zip, unzip over the same folder, then click reload on HAULS in chrome://extensions. If Chrome mentions developer mode extensions, keep it enabled.

Install in a minute

No store needed. Works on a computer with Chrome, Edge or Brave.

  1. Download the zip and unzip it into a folder you'll keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and pin HAULS.
  5. Open a product on Amazon.in or Flipkart. A small HAULS tab appears on the right edge: click it.
Updating: download the new zip, unzip over the same folder, then click the reload icon on HAULS in chrome://extensions. Chrome may show a "disable developer mode extensions" notice: keep it enabled.
